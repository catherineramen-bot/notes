- Cuneiform, clay tablets, roll seals
- Syllabic vs. alphabetic vs. ideograms

## Week 2 - Representing Text and Numbers in Computers
- Understanding how computers represent text and numbers in memory.
- Differences between short-term (volatile) and long-term (non-volatile) memory.
- Number systems: binary, octal, decimal, hexadecimal
## Week 3 - Structure of Text
- Text structure in software development.
- Markup languages, special characters, and escaping.
- Newspapers, ledgers, and digital formats like JSON.
## Week 4 - Manipulating Text & Text Editors
- text manipulation and tools for software developers.
- keyboard mastery, text editors, and search techniques.
- Vim, VS Code, other IDEs
- Regex
## Week 5 - Command Line Interfaces
- Origins: teletype machines; commands short for historical reasons.
- Still in use for ease, access to certain functions, and because of bandwidth
- Protocols: SSH, SCP, SFTP
